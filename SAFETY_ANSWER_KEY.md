# Safety Permit Answer Key (for teacher review)

The Winter 2017 safety test PDF has no answer key, so every answer below was INFERRED from standard school-shop practice. Questions marked **CONFIRM** are asked in the game but NOT graded until you confirm them (a miss never fails a student). To change an answer or confirm a question, edit data-safety.js: flip true/false, and delete the "?" to make it graded. Pass mark: tests of 10+ questions allow 1 miss, shorter tests need a perfect score.

## Safety Orientation
1. Is it mandatory to wear safety glasses when you are operating machinery? **YES**
2. Is it safe to operate machinery when under the influence of drugs or alcohol? **NO**
3. Is it safe to distract others while they are using machinery? **NO**
4. Should you ensure loose clothing, hair, and hoodie strings are tied back and tucked in? **YES**
5. Should you ask the teacher for permission before using a machine? **YES**
6. Should you know where the power button is on a machine before using it? **YES**
7. Is it required to wear closed toe shoes in the lab or centre? **YES**
8. Is it safe to leave spilled liquid until the end of class? **NO**
9. Should you use your hands to clean up dust and debris? **NO**
10. Is it safe to use any hand tool for any job? **NO**

## Band Saw  (gates in-game: band_saw)

6. Should you do relief cuts for tight curved cuts on the band saw? **YES**
7. Is it safe to operate machinery when under the influence of drugs or alcohol? **NO**
8. Is it safe to allow your fingers to be closer than 5 cm (2 inches) from the blade? **NO**
9. Should you use excessive force when pushing the wood into the blade of the band saw? **NO**

11. Is it safe to distract others while they are using the band saw? **NO**
12. If your fingers become closer than 5 cm (2 inches) to the blade, should you use a push stick? **YES**
13. Should you use your hands to remove scrap pieces that are closer than 5 cm (2 inches) from the blade? **NO**
14. Should you wear safety glasses when you are operating the band saw? **YES**
15. Is it best to ensure loose clothing, hair, and hoodie strings are tied back and tucked in? **YES**
